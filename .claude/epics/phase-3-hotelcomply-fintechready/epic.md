## Products

### HotelComply

Industry: Boutique hotels, guesthouses (5,000+ in Nigeria)  
Core Problem: Multiple government agencies, different deadlines, closure risk

**Features:**
- Tourism licence expiry calendar with pre-renewal alerts
- Fire safety certificate status tracker (inspection history)
- Hygiene inspection record
- Environmental permit (NESREA) tracker
- **VAT return due-date calendar** (21st of each month — WhatsApp reminder)
- Monthly PAYE remittance tracker
- Staff NSITF and pension compliance board
- **Claude-generated FIRS audit preparation checklist**
- **Guest privacy policy generator** (NDPA-compliant)
- Free "Hotel Compliance Score" audit PDF as lead magnet

**Claude AI Use Case:**
- Input: Hotel manager inputs property type, staff count, services, current permits
- Output: Compliance status report with traffic-light for each regulation, next 5 deadlines, penalty exposure, prioritised action plan

**Pricing:**
- Guesthouse: ₦30,000/mo
- Hotel: ₦55,000/mo
- CaaS Premium: ₦150,000/mo

**Sales Strategy:** Walk-in visits with printed free Hotel Compliance Score audit — physical approach works in hospitality.

**Target:** 20 hotels = ₦600,000 MRR

### FintechReady

Industry: CBN-licensed fintech startups (3,360+)  
Core Problem: CBN cybersecurity audits, NDPA GAID registration, AML/KYC compliance

**Why Last:** Highest value (₦1.6M MRR potential) but requires most trust. Need 5 products, 100+ clients, credible brand before launching.

**Features:**
- **CBN cybersecurity self-assessment** (mapped to 2024 framework)
- Annual audit preparation checklist (all CBN documentation)
- **Claude-generated incident response plan template**
- 24-hour breach notification workflow (NDPC + CBN report templates)
- Quarterly CBN compliance report deadline calendar
- **NDPC DCPMI registration tracker** and status dashboard
- **Claude-generated annual data protection audit report**
- KYC policy suite (onboarding, transaction limits, enhanced due diligence)
- AML transaction monitoring policy templates
- Data subject rights request management system

**Claude AI Use Case:**
- Input: Fintech founder inputs licence type, staff count, monthly transaction volume, infrastructure
- Output: Full CBN self-assessment with compliance score, critical gaps (fix before exam), moderate gaps (90 days), estimated fine exposure, 12 required policy documents generated

**Pricing:**
- Early Stage: ₦80,000/mo
- Licensed Fintech: ₦150,000/mo
- CaaS Enterprise: ₦400,000/mo (+ dedicated compliance specialist, monthly call, board-ready reports)

**Target:** 20 clients = ₦1,600,000 MRR
